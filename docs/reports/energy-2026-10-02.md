## The late-frame question

The 4K60 clip on the desktop, the probe on. Seams first, since a late seam picture is the black flash the roadmap names: none.

| Run | Loops | Over 1.5 frame durations at a seam | Mid-pass | Largest gap | Seam step | Seam lead |

| The soak, 2026-09-28 | 220 | 1 | 133 | 2.12 | 1.00 | 1.14 s |
| The late-frame row, 2026-10-02, apps open | 60 (47 seams watched) | 0 | 15 | 8.86, mid-pass | 1.00 | 1.19 s |
| The quiet energy runs, 2026-10-02 | 340 | 0 | 257 | 14.65, mid-pass | 1.00 | 1.13 s |

**Accepted as load, in writing.** The Mac was never idle: with the apps quit, WindowServer still sat at 44 % compositing the wallpaper and the terminal, and the 340-loop count spans pauses, covering and display sleep, whose gaps the probe counts mid-pass. The engine's own numbers say the pictures were queued on time: the seam step is 1.00 throughout, the seam lead over a second, no flushes, no failures, no next-reader misses, and 177 renderer drops in 102,134 frames (0.17 %). A clean idle machine was not to be had here; M10-1.0.md's loop row (20 loops of an optimised copy, no gap over 1.5 at any seam) is the check that carries on.
