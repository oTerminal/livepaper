# M5: production engine, supervisor and render host

Turn the spike's engine into product code: the gapless loop, the layer tree of a surface, the playback supervisor, the wallpaper extension that WallpaperAgent hosts, and the app's client for it. Lane A. See `docs/roadmap.md` (Engineering decisions, Architecture) and decision records 0001 to 0004; the evidence behind them is in `Spikes/results/`.

Its inputs are settled: record 0001 (the host is the extension; gate G1 passed), 0002 (the library and the notifications), 0003 (stopping holds a still), and M2-core.md's seams, which this milestone uses and does not re-implement: `decidePlayback`, `judgeProgress`, `judgeHeartbeat`, `allowAgentRestart`, `pictureRect`, `RenderState`, `Heartbeat`, `LibraryLocation`.

## Rules

- Code goes in `LivepaperPlayback` (nonisolated), `LivepaperSystem` (MainActor), a new `WallpaperAgentBridge` target in `LivepaperKit`, and the extension target in `WallpaperExtension/`, declared in `targets/extension.yml` and included from `project.yml` as M0 planned. The App target gets only what the checks below need.
- All private API lives in `WallpaperAgentBridge`: every `dlopen`, class name, selector and ivar write, the request-field extraction, and the one private selector on the video layers. The extension links it; nothing else does. It starts with a self-check that logs one line naming whatever is missing, and the heartbeat carries the result (`selfCheckFailed`).
- The extension reads the library and nothing else (record 0002). Every path it opens comes from a `LibraryPath` through `LibraryLocation`; the home folder comes from `getpwuid`, in one place. It writes no file: the heartbeat is all it says.
- Nothing blocks on the cooperative pool (M4-import.md, "As built"). `LoopEngine` is an actor whose executor is its own `DispatchSerialQueue`; the readers' blocking calls run there and nowhere else. The XPC handler hops to the main actor and returns.
- Nothing polls while nothing changes. The displayed-picture counter runs for the seconds a check needs (after a wake, after a recovery, when asked) and stops. The heartbeat is one timer.
- Vocabulary is `CONTEXT.md`'s: surface, display, render host, poster, loop seam. A type called `Screen`, `Monitor`, `Renderer` or `Thumbnail` is a review failure.
- Anything adapted from Phosphene (MIT) keeps its attribution in a `NOTICE` at the repository root, as `Spikes/NOTICE` does. No GPL project is read.
- Test-first at the seams below. What touches AVFoundation or the agent is proved by the checks table, on screen, with the log kept.
- The extension's bundle identifier is what the wallpaper store keys the user's selection by, and the choice identifier `livepaper` is the configuration it carries (records 0001 and 0003; M7-system-integration.md writes it). Both are chosen here, once, and never change.

## What the spike settled

- **The loop** (`Spikes/results/S2.md`). Two `AVAssetReader`s on the video track, `AVAssetReaderTrackOutput` with no output settings, compressed samples straight into the layer's `AVSampleBufferVideoRenderer`, a second reader opened ahead, one ever-increasing timeline. A frame is a buffer with at least one sample and a data buffer; the reader's marker buffers are left out. Every sample is stamped with its output (edit-list-adjusted) time plus the pass's offset; the next pass starts at `end of last frame − PTS of first frame`; `ResetDecoderBeforeDecoding` is stripped from every pass after the first. The first frame goes in with the clock stopped and `DisplayImmediately`. A renderer that fails or asks for a flush restarts from a fresh reader, because the reader is mid-GOP. A layer inherited from an earlier engine is flushed first. 200 loops with no presented gap over 1.5 frame durations, in a window and in the extension.
- **Progress is displayed pictures**: `displayedPixelBuffer()`, told apart by the `IOSurface` ID behind it, polled. Not the timebase and not `AVVideoPerformanceMetrics`; neither saw the seam defect.
- **Switching in place** (S7): one serial queue, the last request wins, requests that arrive during the renderer's flush coalesce into one restart. 50 switches in 10 s ended on the last.
- **Crossfade** (S5): two video layers made up front, the lower one opaque once it has played, only the upper one's opacity animated, by an explicit `CABasicAnimation` so that it runs in the render server. The fade starts when the incoming layer reports `isReadyForDisplay`, not when its first frame is enqueued: 4,881 of 4,881 samples then had a fully visible video.
- **Surfaces** (S3). The agent hands the extension one surface per display, not per Space; the lock screen is the same surface with `presentationMode = locked`; the Settings preview is a second surface for that display, flagged `isPreview`, alive while Settings is open. So the roadmap's "one decode per display, fanned out" is one engine per surface, and the preview is the one time a display costs two. A replug is a new surface ID for the same display UUID; the old surface never returns, so what a returning display shows comes from the UUID, and the 15 s teardown grace only covers the agent's routine reconnects. A monitor the Mac has never seen was not tried.
- **Covered means not composited** (S3). Under a fullscreen app a display's surface shows no new pictures while the engine feeds it. The watchdog must not call that a stall, and it is a first-hand signal that the display is covered.
- **A mode change is stretched, not renegotiated** (S3). The agent sends nothing and scales the old surface into the new size, each axis on its own. The extension has to notice the reconfiguration itself and re-lay; whether the agent then stretches the corrected layer again is for this milestone to find out.
- **The install hazard** (`S0.md`): after the bundle is replaced, the next host-app launch kills the running extension once and the agent does not start it again. `judgeHeartbeat` and a rate-limited agent restart are the cure. Killing the agent is survivable (S7): it comes back, launches the extension once, acquires each surface once.
- **Snapshots** (S6, by construction only): the agent asks the extension for a snapshot and shows it while no live context is hosted. Handing back the picture on screen, cropped as the layer shows it, is what should keep a lock transition from going grey. Nobody has watched it yet.
- **With no app running** (record 0003) the extension reads the last render state itself, holds each display's poster when the state is stopped, keeps what it shows when the state cannot be read, and shows a neutral colour when there is none.
- **The heartbeat's `desktopSurfaceAcquired` flag** is how the app learns that Livepaper is selected (S8b). Selecting it is M7-system-integration.md's.

## As built

What M5 found out or decided on the way, for M6-screens.md and M7-system-integration.md to build on, and the log wording that M8-hardening.md's parser and M10-1.0.md's checklist read. Every wording is quoted from the code; `<…>` is a field.

### The extension and the app

- **Identity** (`WallpaperExtensionIdentity` in `LivepaperCore`, chosen once). Bundle identifier `app.livepaper.Livepaper.WallpaperExtension`, choice identifier `livepaper`, extension point `com.apple.wallpaper`. System Settings lists one group with one item, "Livepaper", described as "Your live wallpaper" (an English constant), with the tile from the extension's bundle.
- **Notifications** (`HostNotification`, Darwin notifications with 64 bits of state, record 0002):
  - `app.livepaper.render-state` (`renderStateChanged`), app to extension: `render-state.json` was replaced. No state.
  - `app.livepaper.heartbeat` (`heartbeat`), extension to app: a packed `Heartbeat`. The low 32 bits are the render state's generation; the high 32 are the flags `desktopSurfaceAcquired` (1), `holdingStill` (2), `spiralDetected` (4), `selfCheckFailed` (8) and `restartAgentRequested` (16).
  - `app.livepaper.recover` (`recover`), app to extension: a `RecoveryLevel`'s raw value, 0 `flush`, 1 `rebuildSurface`, 2 `rebuildPipeline`. `restartAgent` (3) is the app's own and never sent; the extension logs any other state and ignores it.
  - `app.livepaper.check` (`check`), app to extension: run the watchdog's check now. No state.
  - `app.livepaper.playback-metrics` (`playbackMetrics`), app to extension: 1 switches the probe on, anything else off. The extension also reads the state at launch. The app posts 0 when it activates, and again when it deactivates with the probe on, so the probe lasts one session.
- **The heartbeat** beats at launch, every `HeartbeatTiming.standard.interval`, after each change of render state, after an acquire or invalidate that changes it, and on a spiral. It is not logged.
- **`PreviewPlayer`** (`LivepaperPlayback`), `@MainActor public final class PreviewPlayer: NSView`:
  - `public init(logger: Logger, presentation: Presentation = Presentation())`, with the app's logger.
  - `public var presentation: Presentation`: a change lays the picture out again.
  - `public var volume: Double`, 0 to 1, default 0, at which the audio track is not opened.
  - `public private(set) var url: URL?`: what it plays, or will once it can be seen.
  - `public func play(_ url: URL)`: from the first frame, at once if the view can be seen, otherwise when it can. The URL already playing is a no-op.
  - `public func stop()`: forgets the URL and stops the engine.
  - `public func setMetricsProbe(_ on: Bool)`: the metrics line for `preview`.
  - Hidden, or out of its window, it hides its layer and stops its engine (`LoopEngine.stop()`: readers released, picture taken off the layer). Shown again, it starts from the first frame. From nothing the layer stays clear until it has a picture; a switch keeps the last one up until the next is there. A video it cannot play leaves the black background, which is also Fit's bars. When the view goes, its engine is retired.
- **Resuming after a relaunch.** `render-state.json` on disk is how the app picks up where it left off: at launch it reads the file back, gives each display the wallpaper, presentation and volume it had (less wallpapers that have left the library) and applies that live, the generation continuing. A pause by the user is not restored.
- **The developer menu** (`App/Developer/`): a status line, Import Video…, Set Wallpaper (on all displays, on one, or in a preview window), Presentation (Fill, Fit, Stretch; focal point centre or off centre), Volume (0 or 0.5), Pause All, Resume, Restart Wallpaper Service, Run Watchdog Check, Log Playback Metrics, Checks (Crossfade Back and Forth ×6, 50 Switches in 10 s) and Quit Livepaper. M6 removes all of it but "Log Playback Metrics", which M8's soak and M10's checklist switch on.

### Decisions

- **Host status** (`HostStatusReducer`). `.connecting` until the first heartbeat since activation; then `.live` or `.notSelected` by `desktopSurfaceAcquired`, and `.unavailable` on `selfCheckFailed`; `.recovering(level)` in silence; `.stopped` after `deactivate`.
- **One restart, then wait for a heartbeat.** After the agent is restarted, no automatic restart (`silence`, `spiral`, `watchdog`) is made again until a heartbeat follows the last one. When Livepaper is not selected the agent never launches the extension, and restarting it every ten minutes would only redraw the desktop. The status stays `.recovering(restartAgent)` and no timer runs. The status line's button (`user`) is held by the ten-minute gap alone.
- **The gap outlives the app.** Every restart goes through `allowAgentRestart` with `agentRestartGap` (600 s, defined once, in `LivepaperCore`). The last restart is kept in the app's defaults under `app.livepaper.host.lastAgentRestart`, so a relaunch does not open the gap early; one recorded later than now counts as made now.
- **A wake starts a new grace period.** The heartbeat from before the sleep says nothing about the extension after it. A check that falls due in a sleep fires at the wake, before the wake is heard or with the sleep never heard, so any event more than half a step after the check that was due counts as a wake first: a timer on an awake Mac is late by a fraction of a second.
- **Before the first heartbeat, straight to the restart.** Silence before any heartbeat since activation is the install hazard: the extension is dead, and the levels it would try in its own process have nothing to receive them. They are skipped, each said in the log, and the agent is restarted at grace plus one step (30 s), where the ladder would have reached `rebuildSurface`. The user chose this. After a heartbeat, silence climbs the whole ladder: `flush` when the last heartbeat expires, a level every `step` after that, the restart three steps on.
- **`HeartbeatTiming.standard` is measured** on the product extension, macOS 27.0: interval 5 s, grace 20 s, lifetime 15 s, step 10 s. Heartbeats came every 5.00 s (4.80 to 5.11 s over 4 minutes), one 13 ms after the app applied a state, and one 0.07 s after an agent restart. The hazard recovered with the desktop live 30.6 s after launch. The grace stays at 20 s to leave the extension room at login, which M8 measures.
- **Covered displays** (`coveredDisplays(windows:displays:ignoringOwners:)`). A display is covered when one on-screen window at the normal level or above contains its frame, less the strip beside a camera housing. There a fullscreen app starts below the menu bar, which ends a point below the strip (`ConnectedDisplay.topSafeAreaInset`, from `NSScreen.safeAreaInsets.top`: 38 pt and a 39 pt menu bar on a MacBook at 1800×1169, macOS 27), and the strip is black: the desktop is not composited at all. The app's own windows and the Dock's (a display-sized window above the normal level on every display) are ignored by owner PID, the Dock's found by its bundle identifier on each read; window names are never read. So are loginwindow's, found the same way: its lock screen shield is display-sized above the normal level and stays listed for about a second after the unlock, which paused the unlocked display, and the lock screen is the lock sensor's business anyway. Several windows that tile a display do not cover it, v1's known gap, and nor does a zoomed window, which leaves the menu bar. With the Dock hidden, a zoomed window below a camera housing has a fullscreen window's bounds, so it counts although the menu bar still shows a strip of wallpaper, and the wallpaper pauses under it. The window list is read 0.25 s and 1 s after an event that can change it, never on a timer. The Space change comes as the fullscreen animation ends, and both reads see the fullscreen window.
- **No conditions until something is sensed.** `ConditionsFolder` gives nothing until what is sensed differs from its defaults (nothing covered or asleep, unlocked, no Low Power Mode, on mains power). A quiet Mac's render states carry no conditions, which plays the same. While a rule would pause a display, the same conditions are given again every 15 s, half `PlaybackConditions.expiry`.
- **Every surface of a display follows its decision**, the Settings preview included. A surface within its 15 s teardown grace follows new render states too, so a stopped state releases its decoders and a re-acquire finds it up to date.
- **The screen saver counts as locked.** The agent's `idle` is taken as `locked`: shown above every window, so the watchdog still judges it on a covered display. A mode the bridge does not know keeps the surface's mode.
- **What the watchdog judges.** Live, playing surfaces that are not the preview, on a display neither asleep nor covered; on the lock screen covering does not count. The restart flag clears when the surface that asked is judged healthy, invalidated or torn down. A surface that shows fewer than half the expected pictures while its engine fed the renderer at least half the expected frames over the same window is `notComposited`, not stalled, since "under a fullscreen app a display's surface shows no new pictures while the engine feeds it. The watchdog must not call that a stall" (What the spike settled): nothing is tried, and its ladder and the restart flag stay as they were, so a covered display the render state does not know of (the app dead or late, a gap in its sensing) no longer climbs to an agent restart.
- **The mode change.** On the built-in display (macOS 27.0) the agent did send something: switching 1800×1169@2 to 1920×1200@1 and back came as an `update` carrying the new size (`bridge: update surface … size 1920x1200@1.0`), and the extension re-laid the surface from it (`extension: surface resized surface=<id> from=1800x1169@2.0 to=1920x1200@1.0`). The agent did not stretch the corrected layer: the 4:3 wallpaper in Fit measured 160 pt bars and exactly 4:3 at 1920×1200, and 120.5 pt at 1800×1169 (`pictureRect`: 160 and 120.7). For displays where the agent sends nothing, as S3 saw on an external one, the extension also takes CoreGraphics' reconfiguration callback, waits 0.5 s, compares the display's geometry with the one recorded at acquire and hands a new one to `PlaybackSupervisor.layout(display:geometry:)`; it logged nothing here, the update having come first. An external display was not available for M5's checks.
- **Audio at the seam.** At volume 0.5 a 440 Hz tone clip was audible with no click at the seam (heard on the built-in speakers), and the extension ran an audio pipeline (`audiomentor`, `audioqueue.source`, `audio.IOThread.client`, `AQConverterThread` threads). At 0 none of those threads exist: the audio track is not opened. A change of volume takes effect at the next loop seam.
- **Stopping and the decoder.** Nothing in the layer's or the renderer's public API releases the hardware decoder while the layer stays: flushing, removing the renderer from the synchroniser, releasing the synchroniser, a nil timebase, hiding or unparenting the layer all left one session and about 41 MB of buffers, and a renderer that has been pulled from outlives its layer, so replacing the layer would not help either. What releases it is one tiny JPEG sample marked DoNotDisplay, enqueued after the flush under a format description of the video's size (`DecoderRelease.swift`): the session goes and the picture on the layer stays where it was. `stop()`, `suspend()`, holding a still and showing nothing do it; `pause()` keeps the decoder, as a covered display should. On screen the extension's decoder service went from one `AVDFrameReceiver` thread and 24 MB of IOSurfaces while playing to none and 16 KB a minute after Pause All and Quit. The `VTDecoderXPCService` process itself never exits (it idles at a few MB), so "gone" in the Stopped check means no session and no buffers.
- **Developer notes.** Every `make build` in another worktree registers another copy of the extension under the same bundle identifier, and the agent then launches whichever it likes; `pluginkit -m -v -D -i app.livepaper.Livepaper.WallpaperExtension` lists them and `pluginkit -r <path>` removes one. The extension's launch heartbeat goes out before the agent acquires a surface, so the status reads `.notSelected` for about 0.15 s after every extension launch (M6's status line may want to wait one heartbeat). Conditions sensed before a sleep expire during it, so at wake a display can go `play`, `suspend`, `play` within 0.2 s before its display is lit (M8's lid cycles watch it).

### Log lines

The extension logs under `app.livepaper.extension` (`WallpaperExtensionIdentity.logSubsystem`), categories `bridge`, `extension`, `supervisor` and `surface` (layer trees and engines, the metrics line among them). The app logs under `app.livepaper.Livepaper` (`LivepaperSystem.logSubsystem`), categories `host`, `sensing`, `developer` and `preview`. The package's tests log under `app.livepaper.tests`, so neither subsystem shows their lines. Every line is `.public`, and `.notice` unless marked. The wording lives in `SelfCheck.swift`, `BridgeLog.swift`, `SupervisorLog.swift`, `LogLines.swift` (`HostLog`), `ExtensionLog.swift` and `PlaybackMetrics.swift`; `SelfCheckTests`, `SupervisorLogTests`, `HostLogTests` (the ladder, the restarts and the status) and `PlaybackMetricsTests` pin it. Below, `<level>` is `flush`, `rebuildSurface` or `rebuildPipeline`.

- **Bridge self-check** (`bridge`), one line at launch: `bridge self-check: all present`, `bridge self-check: usable, missing: <names>` (`.error`) or `bridge self-check: failed, missing: <names>` (`.error`). `<names>` is a comma-separated list from `WallpaperExtensionKit`, a payload class name, `+[CAContext remoteContextWithOptions:]`, `-[CAContext contextId layer setLayer:]`, `-[CAContext invalidate]`, `WallpaperRemoteContextXPC.box`, `WallpaperSnapshotXPC.rawValue` and `-[AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer _setDisallowsVideoLayerDisplayCompositing:]`. `failed` means no surface can be acquired; the heartbeat then carries `selfCheckFailed`. The bridge also logs each request from the agent (`bridge: acquire surface <UUID> …`) and `bridge: spiral detected, <n> empty connections in a row; the app is asked to restart WallpaperAgent` (`.error`).
- **Surface fields.** Every supervisor line about one surface carries `<F>`: `surface=<surface UUID> display=<display UUID> preview=<true|false> wallpaper=<wallpaper UUID|none> generation=<n|none>`.
- **Surface events** (`supervisor`): `acquire new <F>`, `acquire reused <F>`, `invalidate <F>`, `invalidate unknown surface=<surface UUID>`, `torn down <F>`, `now showing <F> crossfade=<true|false>`, `holding still <F>`, `showing nothing <F>`, `update <F> mode=<desktop|locked>`.
- **Decisions and the render state** (`supervisor`): `decision display=<display UUID> decision=<play|pause.<reason>|suspend.<reason>|still|nothing> generation=<n|none>` when a display's decision changes, `<reason>` one of `user`, `desktopCovered`, `displayAsleep`, `displayLocked`, `lowPowerMode`, `onBattery`; `render state read generation=<n> stopped=<true|false> displays=<k>`; `render state missing, showing nothing`; `render state unreadable, keeping generation=<n|none>: <reason>`.
- **Watchdog verdicts and the ladder in the extension** (`supervisor`): `check started reason=<wake|unlock|recovery|update|check> surfaces=<k>`; `check count <F> displayed=<n> expected=<m> fed=<f>`, `<f>` being the frames the engine fed the renderer between the same polls; `check verdict <F> verdict=<healthy|notComposited|flush|rebuildSurface|rebuildPipeline|restartAgent> attempt=<k>`, `<k>` being the recoveries already tried; `recovery tried <F> level=<level>`; `restart requested <F>`; `restart request cleared`; `recover requested level=<level> stalled=<k>` for the app's `recover`.
- **The extension** (`extension`): `extension: launched pid=<pid> version=<version> build=<build> bundle=<path>`; `extension: recover received level=<level>`; `extension: recover ignored state=<n>`; `extension: check received`; `extension: playback metrics probe <on|off>`; `extension: display reconfigured display=<display UUID> from=<geometry|none> to=<geometry>`, a geometry being `<w>x<h>@<scale>` in points.
- **The host's ladder** (app, `host`): `host: activated, waiting for the extension's heartbeat`; `host: status <stopped|connecting|notSelected|live|recovering(<level or restartAgent>)|unavailable>`; `host: no heartbeat, asking the extension to <level>`; `host: no heartbeat since activation, skipping <level>: no extension to receive it`; `host: asking the extension to <level>` for the app's own `recover`; `host: check requested`; `host: playback metrics <on|off>`; `host: render state <n> written` or `host: render state <n> written, stopped` (`.info`).
- **Restarts** (app, `host`): `host: restarting WallpaperAgent (<silence|spiral|watchdog|user>)`; `host: WallpaperAgent restarted, pid <old> -> <new>`; `host: WallpaperAgent pid <old> signalled, not back yet`; `host: WallpaperAgent is not running, nothing to restart`; `host: could not signal WallpaperAgent pid <pid>, errno <errno>`; `host: not restarting WallpaperAgent (<reason>): restarted less than 10 minutes ago, allowed from <ISO 8601 time>`; `host: not restarting WallpaperAgent (<reason>): no heartbeat since the last restart`. A restart is one `SIGTERM`, then a look every 250 ms for up to 5 s.
- **The metrics line** (`surface` in the extension; the preview's under the logger it was given), every 20 loops while the probe is on: `playback metrics for <subject> on <folder>/<file>: loops <n>, seams watched <n>, seam step <x.xx|none>, in-loop step <x.xx|none>, presented gap <x.xx|none>, at a seam <x.xx|none>, gaps over 1.5 at seams <n>, elsewhere <n>, renderer dropped <n|none> of <n|none>, seam lead <x.xx|none> s, flushes <n>, failures <n>, markers skipped <n>, next-reader misses <n>`. `<subject>` is `surface <surface UUID>` or `preview`; steps and gaps are in frame durations; for a library video `<folder>` is the wallpaper's UUID.
